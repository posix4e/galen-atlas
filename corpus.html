The corpus

Every work we track, with the language it survives in and what we know about its English translation. Read this as a record of what has been looked up, not as a reference work: each row states how its status was established, and for most of them the honest answer is that nothing was consulted at all. Those rows rest on a language model's recollection and may be wrong in either direction. Rows marked as found in a catalogue were checked against the automatically refreshed BBAW/CMG snapshot — the listing was seen, the translation itself was not. Nothing here has been verified against a publication. Corrections welcome, and they are the point.
